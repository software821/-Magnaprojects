FAQ

The questions everyone asks.

What is Boulevard?

Boulevard is an AI music app. It is the AI alternative to Spotify. Tap a vibe, get a song nobody has heard before. Every track is generated end to end by AI and screened by a human before it ships. No Drake. No Taylor. No licensed catalog. Free on iOS and Android.

Is Boulevard really an AI music app?

Yes. Every track on Boulevard is composed end-to-end by an AI model. There is no human band, no licensed catalog. Each song is then listened to by a human before it ships, so quality stays high.

Is Boulevard a Spotify alternative?

Yes. Boulevard is the AI alternative to Spotify. Spotify licenses songs from labels and hands you a billion of them. Boulevard generates songs from scratch, tuned to how you feel. Smaller catalog. Stranger artists. Yours alone.

How is Boulevard different from Suno, Udio, or Stable Audio?

Suno, Udio, and Stable Audio are AI music generators. You type a prompt, they make a song you download. Boulevard is an AI music app. You pick a vibe, you listen. We do the prompting, screening, and curation for you. Think Spotify, not Photoshop.

Is Boulevard free?

Yes. Boulevard is a free download. No subscription required. There is a daily listening cap on the free tier.

Can I listen without making an account?

Yes. You can listen anonymously from the moment the app opens. Signing in with Apple, Google, or Facebook syncs your library and taste profile across devices.

Are AI-generated songs copyrighted?

In the United States, the U.S. Copyright Office has stated that purely AI-generated works are not eligible for copyright protection (see our full article on this). Boulevard owns its catalog as a curated product. You can listen freely; commercial reuse of individual tracks requires a license from us.

What devices does Boulevard support?

Boulevard runs on iPhone (iOS 15 or later) and Android (Android 8 or later). Tablets are not supported in the first release. We are an iOS & Android-first app.

Does Boulevard work in the United States?

Yes. Boulevard is built for the U.S. market first, with U.S. English UI and trending content tuned for American listeners. The app works globally, but our content team is in the U.S. and our defaults reflect that.

What kinds of music does Boulevard generate?

Lo-fi, focus, late-night R&B, indie folk, synthwave, ambient, hip-hop, house, acoustic, chillhop, drill, bossa nova, dream pop, jazz, and more. We add genres weekly based on what our listeners actually save.

Do you store my data?

We store only what is needed to make the app work. Your taste profile, listening events, and (if you sign in) an account identifier. We do not sell personal information. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
