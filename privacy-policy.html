Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Boulevard is an AI-native music platform that allows users to discover, stream, personalize, save, and interact with AI-generated music and artists.

This Privacy Policy explains how Boulevard collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Boulevard mobile application, website, and related services.

By using Boulevard, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

A. Account Information

When you create or upgrade an account, we may collect:

  • Email address
  • Username
  • Profile information
  • Authentication provider information (Google, Apple, Facebook, TikTok)
  • User ID and account identifiers

Anonymous accounts may also be created automatically for app functionality and personalization.

B. Listening & Activity Data

We collect information about how you use Boulevard, including:

  • Songs played
  • Listening duration
  • Replays
  • Skips
  • Saves
  • Shares
  • Likes and reactions
  • Comments and replies
  • Playlist activity
  • Follows
  • Search activity
  • Session behavior
  • Personalized recommendation signals

This data helps power Boulevard's recommendation systems, personalization engine, moderation systems, analytics, and product improvements.

C. User-Generated Content

We collect content you create inside Boulevard, including:

  • Comments
  • Replies
  • Reactions
  • Playlist names
  • Profile content
  • Reports and support requests

You are responsible for the content you submit.

D. Device & Technical Data

We may collect:

  • Device identifiers
  • Push notification tokens
  • IP address
  • App version
  • Operating system information
  • Language and region settings

3. AI-Generated Content

Boulevard contains AI-generated music, artist personas, artwork, metadata, and related content.

Artists, songs, visuals, and creative identities on Boulevard may be fictional or algorithmically generated.

While Boulevard uses moderation and review systems, generated content may occasionally resemble existing artistic styles, genres, themes, or creative patterns.

Boulevard does not intentionally impersonate real individuals or artists.

4. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide and operate Boulevard
  • Personalize recommendations and feeds
  • Improve recommendation quality
  • Train and optimize ranking systems
  • Enable playlists, comments, and social features
  • Prevent abuse, spam, fraud, and platform manipulation
  • Moderate user-generated content
  • Process subscriptions and purchases
  • Deliver push notifications
  • Analyze product performance
  • Improve music generation systems
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Personalization & Recommendations

Boulevard uses behavioral signals and recommendation systems to personalize your experience.

This may include:

  • Genre preferences
  • Mood preferences
  • Replay behavior
  • Session activity
  • Saved songs
  • Likes and comments
  • Listening patterns
  • Recommendation clusters

These systems may adapt automatically over time.

6. Sharing Information

We may share information with service providers that help operate Boulevard.

These may include:

  • Supabase
  • RevenueCat
  • Expo
  • Cloudflare
  • OpenAI
  • Anthropic
  • AI music generation providers
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers

We do not sell personal information.

Boulevard does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not share personal information with advertising networks or data brokers.

7. Push Notifications

Boulevard may send push notifications related to:

  • Replies
  • Likes
  • Activity
  • Recommendations
  • Product updates
  • Trending content

You can disable notifications in your device settings.

8. Community & Moderation

Boulevard may review, remove, restrict, or moderate:

  • Comments
  • Reactions
  • User-generated content
  • Accounts
  • Activity that violates platform rules

We may suspend or terminate accounts that:

  • Harass others
  • Abuse the platform
  • Attempt fraud or manipulation
  • Violate applicable laws
  • Upload or distribute harmful content

9. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide services
  • Maintain platform integrity
  • Improve systems
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Resolve disputes

Some data may remain in backups or logs for security and operational purposes.

10. Account Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated data.

To request deletion, contact support@boulevardai.app, or use the Delete account page.

Some information may be retained where legally required or necessary for fraud prevention, safety, moderation, or financial compliance.

11. Children

Boulevard is not intended for children under 13.

If we become aware that a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable law, we may remove the information and terminate the account.

12. Security

We use commercially reasonable safeguards to protect information.

However, no system is completely secure, and Boulevard cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. International Users

Your information may be processed and stored in countries outside your jurisdiction.

By using Boulevard, you consent to such transfers where permitted by law.

14. Changes To This Policy

Boulevard may update this Privacy Policy periodically.

Continued use of Boulevard after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
