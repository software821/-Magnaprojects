Why people are leaving Spotify in 2026

It's not one thing. It's five, stacked:

  1. Price. Premium hit $11.99 a month in 2024. Up from $9.99 in 2023. Apple Music is $10.99. Boulevard is.
  2. Podcast bloat. Spotify spent over a billion on exclusive podcasts. Joe Rogan, the Obamas, Gimlet. For users who didn't care, it felt like the music app became something else.
  3. Drake fatigue. Drake is the most-streamed artist in Spotify history. He's also the artist most Americans say they hear too much of. The algorithm serves what's safe.
  4. Royalty politics. After Spotify's 2024 changes, smaller artists report earning less per stream. Many listeners now split: Spotify for the algorithm, Bandcamp for direct support.
  5. AI music apps. A whole new category showed up. Apps like Boulevard generate music tuned to your mood instead of pulling from a label catalog. For background, focus, and sleep, they often do the job better.

The AI music angle

Spotify's response to AI music has been to ship recommendation tools that they call AI: AI DJ, AI Playlist, AI narration. None of these generate music. They pick songs from Spotify's existing licensed catalog. That's a useful product, but it's not AI music. Full breakdown of the difference.

Real AI music apps exist outside Spotify. They generate songs from scratch and don't depend on a label catalog. Boulevard is the AI alternative to Spotify. Tap a vibe. Get a song that fits the moment. Free on iOS and Android.

There's also a less-discussed angle: Spotify itself has been quietly seeded with "ghost artist" tracks, AI-adjacent commissioned music designed to fill mood playlists at lower royalty cost. Full story on Spotify's ghost artists.

How to cancel Spotify cleanly

  1. Export your playlists. Spotify's privacy download includes a CSV of every playlist you own.
  2. Recreate the 2 or 3 playlists you actually use on whichever service you pick next.
  3. Keep Spotify for 30 days while you test the new service. Don't switch overnight.
  4. Cancel before the next billing cycle (Spotify charges through the end of the period, not pro-rata).

For background listening (focus, sleep, workout), install Boulevard alongside whatever you pick. Boulevard's free tier has no audio ads. It covers the half of your listening Spotify was always worst at.
