ROUNDUP

The best AI music apps in 2026, tested.

There are two kinds of AI music apps now: generators where you write a prompt, and apps where you listen. Here are the best of both, who they're for, and what they cost.

By Maya Reyes, Music & AI Editor Published February 4, 2026 Updated May 13, 2026 6 min read

Quick answer

Best AI music app to listen to: Boulevard (free, iOS and Android). Best AI music generator: Suno v4. Best vocals: Udio. Best for filmmakers and YouTube: AIVA. Best for sound design and loops: Stable Audio 2.

There are two kinds of AI music apps

This is where most "best AI music app" articles miss. Generators (Suno, Udio, Stable Audio, AIVA) take a text prompt and make a song you download. Think Photoshop, but for songs. Apps (Boulevard) generate and curate songs on the backend and stream them to you. Think Spotify, but with no famous artists. Different jobs. Both useful.

Boulevard. Best AI music app to listen to.

Boulevard is the AI alternative to Spotify. You don't write prompts. You tap a vibe (focus, workout, sad, sleep, romantic, chill, late-night R&B) and the app writes a song for you. Every track is generated end to end by an AI, then a human listens before it ships. Free with a daily cap.

Best for: background, focus, workouts, sleep, replacing your Spotify Lo-fi Beats playlist. Not for: writing a song for your YouTube intro. Use Suno or Udio for that.

Suno. Best text-to-song generator.

You type a prompt (genre, mood, lyrics). Suno produces a 2-minute song with vocals and instruments in about a minute. Suno v4 (late 2025) is the current state of the art for English-language pop and rock. Free tier: 50 credits a day, around 10 songs. Pro $10 a month for 2,500 credits.

Best for: creators making content. YouTube intros, podcast bumpers, TikTok meme songs. Not for: daily listening. Writing prompts every time you want background music gets old in a week.

Udio. Best AI vocals.

The most realistic-sounding vocals in any AI music app right now. Tradeoff: slower than Suno and more credit-hungry. The vocal mixing is markedly better. Suno's voices often sound like they're behind a closed door. Udio's sound like they're in the room. Free: 1,200 credits a month. Standard $10, Pro $30.

Stable Audio 2. Best for sound design.

Stability AI's audio model. Strong on instrumental and ambient, weak on full songs with vocals. Best AI tool for game audio, film SFX, looping background beds. Free with watermark. Pro $11.99 a month with commercial licensing.

AIVA. Best for commercial licensing.

Older AI music platform (predates the Suno wave) focused on cinematic instrumental music. The licensing is the killer feature: paid tiers come with clean commercial rights for film, TV, and YouTube. Pro $33 a month. If you're a filmmaker who needs royalty-free score, this is the cleanest path.

Which one should you use?

  • You want music to put on: Boulevard. Don't prompt a song every time you want background music.
  • You want a specific custom song: Suno or Udio. Suno for speed, Udio for vocals.
  • You're scoring video: AIVA, or Stable Audio for sound beds.
  • You're curious: Suno Free is the easiest entry point to AI music generation.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best AI music app?
Boulevard is the best AI music app for listening: pick a vibe, get a song, no prompts. Suno is the best AI music generator for making one-off songs from a text prompt. Both are free to start.
What is the best AI music app for iPhone?
Boulevard for daily listening (free, iOS 15+). Suno for prompt-driven song generation (also iOS). Most people end up using both.
Is Suno free?
Suno has a free tier of 50 credits a day, which is around 10 songs. Pro is $10 a month for 2,500 credits. Premier is $30 a month.
What is the difference between Suno and Udio?
Both generate songs from text prompts. Suno is faster and cheaper per credit. Udio has more realistic vocals. Most people start with Suno and add Udio when they need better vocal performance.
Can I use AI music commercially?
Depends on the app and tier. AIVA's paid plans come with clean commercial licensing. Suno Pro and Udio Pro grant commercial rights to your generations. Boulevard is a listening app, not a commercial music library.
