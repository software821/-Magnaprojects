FREE TIER GUIDE

Free AI music apps with no subscription required.

Free in AI music means different things. Some apps are free with credits. Some with ads. One is free with no audio ads at all. Here's the field.

By Maya Reyes, Music & AI Editor Published May 7, 2026 Updated May 13, 2026 5 min read

Quick answer

For free AI music to listen to: Boulevard (free, daily listening cap, no audio ads). For free AI music generation: Suno's 50 credits/day. For free with watermarking: Stable Audio 2. Spotify's free tier is not AI music. It's ads on top of a licensed catalog.

"Free" means three different things

  • Free with a cap. Boulevard's free tier lets you listen for a set time per day, then asks you to upgrade. No ads in the audio.
  • Free with credits. Suno, Udio, and AIVA give you a daily or monthly allowance, then charge for more. Generators only.
  • Free with watermark. Stable Audio 2 free downloads include a watermark over the audio. Practical for sketching. Not for finished use.

Boulevard Free

Boulevard is the AI alternative to Spotify. If your goal is to listen to AI music without paying, this is the cleanest experience in 2026.

  • Free to install. No credit card.
  • Daily listening cap (about 3 to 4 hours, varies by region).
  • No audio ads. No interruptions between tracks.
  • Full vibe library: Focus, Workout, Sleep, Sad, Romantic, Chill, plus genre filters.
  • Save, skip, replay. Same UI as Premium.
  • Personalization kicks in after 20 songs.

The free tier is real free. Not a trial. Most casual listeners stay on the free tier and never run into the cap.

Suno Free

  • 50 credits per day. Around 10 songs.
  • Resets every 24 hours.
  • No commercial rights on free tier.
  • Songs are downloadable as.mp3.

If you want to make AI music for fun, this is the best free tier in the category. If you want to listen to AI music, Suno's free tier runs out fast. 10 songs is one workout session.

Udio Free

  • 1,200 credits per month. Around 150 songs.
  • No commercial rights on free tier.
  • Better vocal quality than Suno.

Stable Audio 2 Free

  • Unlimited generation.
  • All free downloads include a watermark.
  • Mostly instrumental. Weak at vocals.
  • Best for sound design and loops.

AIVA Free

  • 3 downloads per month.
  • No commercial rights on free tier.
  • Strong at cinematic instrumental.

What's not really free AI music

Two things often get listed in "free AI music" articles that don't belong:

  • Spotify Free. Not AI music. Licensed catalog of human songs with audio ads. Spotify's AI features (DJ, Playlist) are recommendation layers on top of the same catalog. See our breakdown.
  • YouTube Music Free. Same. Licensed catalog with ads, plus AI-powered recommendations. Not generated music.

Frequently asked questions

Are there any free AI music apps?
Yes. Boulevard's free tier lets you listen to AI-generated music with a daily cap and no audio ads. Suno (50 credits/day), Udio (1,200 credits/month), Stable Audio 2 (watermarked), and AIVA (3 downloads/month) offer free generation.
What is the best free AI music app for iPhone?
Boulevard is the best free AI music listening app for iPhone. Install, tap a vibe, listen. For generation on iOS, Suno's iOS app has a 50-credit/day free tier.
Is Boulevard's free tier really free?
Yes. No credit card required. No audio ads in the music. The only limit is a daily listening cap (about 3 to 4 hours, varies by region).
Is Suno free forever?
Suno has a permanently-free tier of 50 credits per day. Resets every 24 hours. Around 10 songs at typical credit costs.
Can I use free AI music commercially?
Generally no. Most generator free tiers (Suno, Udio, AIVA, Stable Audio) restrict commercial use. Paid tiers grant commercial rights. Boulevard is a listening app, not a generator. You can't use Boulevard tracks commercially without a license from us.
